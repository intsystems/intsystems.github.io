Konstantin Vladimirovich Rudakov (21 June 1954 – 10 July 2021) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician and Full Member of the Russian Academy of Sciences (2016).
He graduated from the MIPT Faculty of Control and Applied Mathematics in 1978 and from 1982 worked at the Dorodnicyn Computing Centre, where he headed the Department of Intelligent Systems.
He defended his Ph.D. dissertation in 1981 and his Doctor of Science dissertation, "Theory of universal and local constraints for recognition algorithms", in 1992.
He was Deputy Director of the Federal Research Center "Computer Science and Control" of RAS, head of the Intelligent Systems Department at MIPT, and professor of the Department of Mathematical Methods of Forecasting at Moscow State University (from 1997).
From 2019 he was Editor-in-Chief of the journal *Doklady RAN. Mathematics, Informatics, Control Processes*.
Lenin Komsomol Prize (1984), Lomonosov Prize of the 1st degree together with Yu. I. Zhuravlyov (2003).

**Professional interests:** the algebraic approach to the synthesis of correct algorithms based on heuristic information models, pattern recognition.
